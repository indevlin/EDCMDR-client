# Sharing

On **Sharing**, pick a commander. Each section is off until you switch it on, and each can wait
before it appears on the site: none, 1 hour, 12 hours, 1 day or 1 week. The site holds the update
until then. Portraits appear at once.

| Section | What appears on your page |
|---|---|
| Ranks | Pilot ranks and progress, navy ranks, superpower reputation |
| Statistics | Your lifetime statistics, and the leaderboards they place you on |
| Fleet | Your ships, their fittings and engineering |
| Carriers | Your fleet carrier and your squadron's carrier: name, callsign, where it is, docking, services, space and packs, as your journal last showed them (open the carrier's management screen in game to refresh). A squadron carrier also appears on the squadron's page |
| Location | Where you are, and where your ships are stored |
| Engineering | Engineers you know, have been invited by or have unlocked |
| Powerplay | Your power, rank, merits and time pledged |
| Squadron | Your squadron and your rank in it, on its roster |
| Portrait | Your commander's picture (see [[Portraits|Portraits]]) |
| Presence | "In game" while you play, and the game mode |

Your commander's name is always shown. Two extra choices:

- **Include net worth and carrier bank balances.** Frontier's figure counts your credits, ships and modules,
  and any carrier you paid for, at Frontier's own valuation, but never the money in a carrier's bank. Your
  page shows Frontier's figure, adds your own fleet carrier's bank, and shows a squadron carrier's bank
  separately, since that money is the squadron's. A squadron carrier's bank appears only when the squadron's
  leader shares it. Off, all of it stays private.
- **Let my earlier commander names find this page.** Off, an old name leads nowhere.

Your choices are saved and sent as you make them.

## In game

With **Presence** on, your page says "In game", with the mode, while the game is running and you are
in a session, not at the main menu. It turns to offline a few minutes after the client stops
reporting.

## Turning things off

- Switch a section off: the site deletes what it holds for it at once.
- **Forget on EDCMDR…** (on **Commanders**) deletes everything the site holds for that commander.
- Commanders not seen for 180 days stay on the site but leave the leaderboards.

Your Frontier ID never appears on the site. See the [privacy policy](https://edcmdr.com/privacy).
