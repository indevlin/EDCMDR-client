# Portraits

Your page can show your commander's picture, cut from a screenshot of the game.

1. In game, open the **Codex** and its hub, with your commander's tile highlighted.
2. Take a screenshot.
3. In the client, on **Commanders**, press **Portrait…** and pick the screenshot.

ED Commanders cuts the portrait out and keeps only that, never the whole screenshot. Switching
**Portrait** off in **Sharing** deletes it.
