# Squadron leaders

A squadron's leader, once verified (see [[Verify with Frontier|Verify-With-Frontier]]), gets a
**Squadron** tab in the client. From it, set what your squadron's page on ED Commanders shows:

- home system
- power
- supported factions
- recruiting status
- a tagline

Press **Save to EDCMDR** to publish. Members share their own squadron rank by switching **Squadron** on in
**Sharing**; the roster lists those who do.

Pick the commander at the top; the tab shows which squadron that commander leads, and saving changes
that squadron only. With several commanders in different squadrons, each edits its own.

The tab appears only for a leader, as Frontier reported at verification, and goes away when your
journal shows otherwise: you left the squadron, joined another, or were moved off rank 0. If you
became leader after verifying, or the tab is gone and shouldn't be, verify again.

## Renaming your squadron

Rename the squadron, or change its tag, in game as usual. ED Commanders takes the new name from the
first member's journal that shows it (at their next login), and members who haven't logged in since
can't put the old name back.

The tag isn't in the journal; it comes from Frontier. When ED Commanders sees the new name, any
member's client that keeps a Frontier sign-in (**Frontier CAPI** switched on, on the **Integrations**
tab, or EDLD's own CAPI sign-in) confirms the squadron with Frontier by itself, and the new tag
follows. No one needs to verify again. If no member keeps a sign-in, the Squadron tab says so; one
**Verify with Frontier** by any member does the same.
