# EDCMDR client

The EDCMDR client publishes your Elite Dangerous commander to
[ED Commanders](https://edcmdr.com). It reads the journal the game writes on your computer and
sends only the parts of your commander you choose, when you choose. It sits in your system tray.

Nothing is sent until you switch it on. There are no accounts and no passwords.

same settings. You need EDLD or the EDCMDR client, not both.
