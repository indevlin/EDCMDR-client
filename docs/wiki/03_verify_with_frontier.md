# Verify with Frontier

Verifying proves the commander is yours. Verified commanders are marked on the site, appear on the
leaderboards, and a squadron's verified leader can edit its page.

1. On **Commanders**, pick your commander and press **Verify with Frontier…**.
2. Your browser opens ED Commanders, showing a short code. Check it matches the one in the client,
   then continue to Frontier's own sign-in page.
3. Sign in with the Frontier account that plays this commander and allow access.
4. Return to the client. It finishes on its own within a few seconds.

Frontier needs a journal from the last two weeks: if you have not played recently, play a session
first.

What happens: Frontier hands the sign-in to your client, on your computer. The client passes it to
ED Commanders once, which reads your commander and squadron from Frontier and then discards it.
Nothing is kept on the site but the result. To keep the sign-in on your computer for Frontier's
Companion API, see [[Integrations|Integrations]].

**"The Frontier account you signed in with plays a different commander":** you signed in with
another account. Sign out on Frontier's site and verify again.

**Changed squadron, or became its leader?** Verify again: the site takes your squadron and rank
from Frontier at that moment.
